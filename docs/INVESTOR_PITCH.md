# raviion — Investor One-Pager

## The market

Nigeria is one of the largest and fastest-growing digital payments markets in Africa, but the experience is still fragmented. Customers still jump across multiple apps to do the basics: fund a wallet, buy airtime or data, pay bills, use travel eSIMs, and manage digital payments. The problem is not demand — it is convenience, trust, and product depth.

raviion is building the operating system for everyday digital financial life for Nigerians at home and in the diaspora.

## The product

raviion is a fintech and lifestyle platform that combines:

- Wallets in Naira and USD
- Virtual accounts and virtual cards
- Airtime, data, cable, electricity, exam pins, betting and other utility payments
- International top-up and mobile data products
- eSIM travel data across 190+ countries
- Rewards, referrals and agent distribution
- A full admin and ops console for operations, compliance, pricing and growth

The experience is delivered as an installable web app today, with a native mobile app path already designed into the product architecture.

## Why this matters

Customers want one app that handles everyday spending, digital utility fulfillment, travel connectivity, and financial control — not five fragmented products stitched together.

raviion solves a real life problem:

- Local users can pay for services and manage money in one place.
- Diaspora Nigerians can support family and manage home-country utilities and digital spending from abroad.
- International users can access eSIM and modern digital finance with fewer friction points.
- Agents and distributors can earn on demand without building their own infrastructure.

## Why raviion can win

### 1. Product breadth creates switching costs

This is not a single-purpose app. It is a financial and lifestyle platform with multiple recurring use cases in the same user journey.

### 2. Strong compliance foundation

The product is designed around tiered KYC, wallet controls, double-entry ledger accounting, secure data handling, and fraud checks. That makes the platform more resilient, more fundable, and more credible with partners.

### 3. Infrastructure designed for scale

The platform uses a provider abstraction model, a modular ledger and transaction engine, and an admin toolkit that enables real-time pricing and operations without engineering bottlenecks. That reduces time-to-market for new products and countries.

### 4. Distribution advantage

The business model includes referrals, rewards, promotions and an agent ecosystem, enabling acquisition through community and partner channels instead of pure paid marketing.

## Business model

raviion monetizes in multiple ways:

- Transaction fees on utility and digital purchases
- FX and card-related fees
- Wallet and account monetization
- Marketplace margin on eSIM and catalogue sales
- Promotions and distribution economics
- Agent commissions and network growth

This creates layered revenue streams rather than a single dependency on one product category.

## Market opportunity

Nigeria and the wider African diaspora represent a large, underserved digital finance and utility market. The user pain points are consistent:

- fragmented digital payment rails
- limited product depth in one app
- lack of trust and transparency
- friction around cross-border payments and travel connectivity
- limited access to international digital experiences for local users

raviion sits at the intersection of fintech, payments, digital commerce and daily utility spend.
